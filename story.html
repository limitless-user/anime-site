運命と時間の糸 The Braided Threads of Fate & Memory

Mitsuha and Taki are total strangers living completely different lives—she in a quiet mountain village, he in the bustling neon sprawl of Tokyo.

When a mysterious wish causes them to randomly swap bodies in their sleep, they begin communicating through notes and rules left in their phones. But as a once-in-a-thousand-years comet approaches Earth, they uncover a truth that bends time, memory, and spatial boundaries.

"A masterpiece of distance, longing, and traditional mythology."

Directed by Makoto Shinkai, Your Name. elevates a simple body-swapping setup into a poetic exploration of Japanese mythology (*Musubi*), tragic disaster, and subconscious connection.
